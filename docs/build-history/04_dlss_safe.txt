Edge of Eternity VR - stage build 04: build 03 + works with the game's DLSS setting on (2026-10-28, test 023) - CURRENT

Install: copy version.dll next to EdgeOfEternity.exe (delete it to uninstall). Delete an old config.txt to get the
current keys (a commented config.txt and eoevr.log are written in the game folder). SteamVR must be running.

- Native per-eye stereo, head tracking (F9 recenter), level horizon (level_camera = 1, View page of the menu).
- HUD on its own panel (F10 on/off). Loading screens on a larger panel.
- Settings menu: hold L3+R3 or press Insert. Pages View / Display / HUD / Debug. Changes save to config.txt.
- Resolution: Display > Resolution (70 / 85 / 100 / 120 / 140 % of SteamVR's recommended size, or Custom).
- Monitor shows the left eye; the game's own flat render is skipped (faster).
- The game's DLSS is switched off while VR runs (it is built for one window and broke stereo). DLSS for VR is being
  worked on separately. Eyes render at full headset resolution - use the Resolution presets for performance.

Please look at: comfort / world scale (View > World scale), level horizon on/off, HUD distance/size/height,
grass at the edges of the view, battles and cutscenes, the gamepad (game + menu), fps at your resolution.
F11 saves the headset images (eoevr_dump_*.bmp) if something looks wrong; eoevr.log has the details.
